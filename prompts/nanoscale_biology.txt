A cryo-electron tomography simulation of a geometric DNA-origami nanobot suspended in a viscoelastic, non-Newtonian fluid. The nanobot uses mechanical arms to refold an amorphous, misfolded protein blob into a beta-sheet helix. As its arms move rapidly, the surrounding fluid undergoes localized shear-thickening, forming visible, temporary gel-like fractures around the moving parts, accurately depicting low-Reynolds-number fluid dynamics.
